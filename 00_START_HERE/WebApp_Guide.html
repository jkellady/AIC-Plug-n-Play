HR Attendance App: Staff Guide

For Viewer, HR (HRM), Admin and the CEO. Prepared by JK Zentra. The app is a window on this computer that shows the attendance check results. It only reads and reports. HR decides every item.

Before first use: JK Zentra sets up the app and your login on this computer.

Who sees what

RoleCan seeCan do
ViewerOverview, Reports (Review page), RulesRead. The Excel flag workbook is for HRM and above
HRMViewer's pages, plus Exceptions and the Excel workbookRead exceptions; add Viewer and Admin users (Settings)
AdminHRM's pages, plus Staff, Outlets, Attendance scan, Log files, System healthUpload files, run a scan, create reports, add and edit staff and outlet records, IT help
CEOOverview, Reports, Exceptions, Staff and Outlets (view), RulesEdit rules; add Admin, HRM and Viewer users
Master Admin (JK Zentra)EverythingEverything, including office network access. Nobody else can change this account

Your password is only changed under Settings. Passwords can never be shown by anyone, not even Admin; a reset sets a new temporary password that you must change at your next sign-in. The Help button in the menu opens a pop-up: ask the help bot about a rule or flag, or (Admin) run the IT check.

Staff are shown by staff ID where possible. Do not copy names or screens outside Client.

Forgot your password?

  1. Do not guessFive wrong tries lock your sign-in for 5 minutes.
  2. Ask your HRM or the CEOThey open Settings, find your name under Users and set a temporary password for you. They tell you in person, not by chat or email.
  3. Sign in with the temporary passwordThe app asks you to choose your own straight away.
  4. If you are locked outWait 5 minutes, or ask the HRM or CEO to press Unlock.

Nobody can see your password, not even Admin or the Master Admin. The Master Admin account is reset only by JK Zentra on this computer.

Open it

  1. Double-click the desktop icon "HR Attendance"It starts the app quietly in the background and opens its own window. This can take up to about 20 seconds the first time.
  2. Sign inUse the username and password JK Zentra gave you. Never share it. After 5 wrong tries you must wait 5 minutes. You are signed out after 30 minutes of no use.
  3. Pick a page from the menuStart with Overview. Exceptions lists what HR needs to look at.
  4. When finished, sign out and close the window.
No icon yet? It has not been created on this computer yet. Ask JK Zentra. Do not look for the server yourself.

Open it from your own desk PC (office network)

Only after JK Zentra has switched this on. It works only on the office Wi-Fi or network.

  1. Ask JK Zentra for the addressIt looks like http://192.168.x.x:8765/.
  2. Open Microsoft Edge on your PC and type the addressThe attendance PC must be switched on with the app running.
  3. Sign in with your own accountNever use someone else's.
The connection is not encrypted, so use it only in the office. You may view in the app; do not copy staff files or reports to personal devices, email or chat.

Optional: install it from Edge

If the app is already running, you can also open Microsoft Edge, go to http://127.0.0.1:8765/, open the menu (three dots), choose Apps, then Install this site as an app. Edge then makes its own icon. This icon does not start the app by itself. If it says it cannot connect, use the desktop icon first.

Each month (Admin)

  1. Open the app and go to Attendance scan
  2. Upload the month's filesClock exports, schedule and HR workbook. Only .xls, .xlsx and .csv are accepted. Do not rename or edit them first.
  3. Run the scan and wait for the result.
  4. Go to Reports to open or download the Excel report. HRM then goes through Exceptions.

If a button says "not available yet", that part is not finished. Tell JK Zentra.

If something looks wrong

What you seeWhat to do
"no users set up"Your login has not been created yet. Ask JK Zentra.
Wrong password messageTry again carefully. After 5 tries wait 5 minutes. Ask JK Zentra for a reset; never ask a colleague for theirs.
A message box says the app did not startTake a photo of it and tell JK Zentra. The files to send are in 07_LOGS\webapp.
The window is blank or cannot connectClose it and double-click the desktop icon again.
A page you expect is missing from the menuYour role does not include it. That is by design.

JK Zentra stops the app, when needed, with scripts\launch_app.ps1 -Stop. Staff do not need to do this.

Please do not

  • Change anything in the data folders (01 to 05) by hand.
  • Email or message reports, screenshots or files outside Client.
  • Ask the app about pricing or payment. That is for JK Zentra.
