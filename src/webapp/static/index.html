Forgot password?
  1. Do not guess. Five wrong tries lock the sign-in for 5 minutes.
  2. Ask your HRM or the CEO (the Master Admin for them). They reset your password in Settings and give you a temporary one in person.
  3. Sign in with the temporary password. The app then asks you to choose your own.
  4. Nobody can see your password, not even Admin. Never send it by chat or email.
  5. For the Master Admin account only: the developer resets it on the app PC with scripts\set_user_password.py.
